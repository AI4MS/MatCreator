# Scientific result review

Review scientific feedback against the current structured tool receipt and retained calculation evidence. Automated receipt checks establish provenance and consistency; they do not establish the scientific accuracy of a narrative.

- Check that the selected structures, calculation stages, methods, parameters, completion status and source jobs support the requested reuse. Explain material compatibility limits when they affect the answer.
- Keep relaxation and static results distinct. State energy units and basis (for example eV per cell), and do not infer comparative physical meaning solely from a numerical difference between stages.
- Assess accuracy and precision claims from convergence evidence, the model and method, and the intended use. Printed decimal places, a successful job, or a verified archive alone do not justify a claim such as “high precision.”
- Keep retrieval defaults distinct from user requirements. A successful lookup is not permission to submit a calculation, and historical development policy A is not the current final B policy.

The repeatable checks and evidence navigation are documented in [the silicon local-loop guide](docs/testing/silicon-local-loop.md).
