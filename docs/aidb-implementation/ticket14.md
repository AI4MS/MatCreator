The same eight-atom Si relaxation record `b7a7c05fa4e583c7fbee1f7abd54b90e`, source frame 2, was used in a fresh normal Web and ADK query, in the saved fully matched candidate dialogue, in the saved real completion dialogue, and in unmodified prototype `4989f1b`. The four scientific summary columns, six detail sections, ordered field labels and values, full JSON, final structure model and role were identical. The candidate includes a reply ordinal and waits for explicit selection to deliver files. Query and completion clicks downloaded EXTXYZ and exposed POSCAR and HTML links. Their EXTXYZ SHA-256 `2a773bb8503aa91cb52228925755fa4b99c90a01ee112378fbfbaefa3be86ddf` also matches the candidate-selection download. Database and sidecar digests were unchanged. The loopback Chromium harness substituted the CDN module at the browser boundary to verify the exact model input. Ticket 01 records rendering with the real MatterViz widget.

Evidence is in `.workspace/aidb-parity14/`, including `parity.json`, `query-history.json`, four screenshots and the reproduction scripts. Commit `4e9d55d` restored the prototype temperature label on non-MD records and confines the temperature evidence field to MD. Failure and recovery evidence from tickets 01, 02, 05, 06 and 13 covers unavailable readback, unverified completion, missing training labels and repeat exports.

A completion-row fault check used the same saved real chat and a clone of its isolated archive. Injecting missing forces after the installed record read made the actual export click show the missing-force reason and offer POSCAR and HTML. Restoring the sidecar and clicking refresh returned the same job, binding and record. The next EXTXYZ was byte-identical to the verified download. The job snapshot, database and sidecar digests and record count matched before and after. See `.workspace/aidb-parity14/completion-faults.json` and `run-faults.sh`.

## Scope

The feature adds query, by-ID detail, export, VASP output archival and managed workflow integration. Repository review found no record editing or deletion, general external import, full AIMD trajectory archival, external process monitor, database migration or training launch in the change. The original Wayfinder decision tickets retain their existing status.
